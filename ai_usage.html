Correct AI Usage

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is taking the world by storm, rapidly changing the way people work, learn, and communicate. Students, business professionals, and employees across various industries are increasingly using AI to generate ideas, expand their knowledge, and improve productivity. One of AI's most significant features is its ability to tailor responses to specific needs, letting users shape the tone, content, and format. As AI improves and becomes more integrated into everyday life, understanding its advantages and potential drawbacks is crucial.

Evaluation of reasoning
The paragraph is clearly written and accurate. AI is changing how people work, learn, and communicate, and the point that users can shape the tone, content, and format of its responses is a real and relevant feature. The closing sentence also points toward a sensible conclusion, that people should understand both the advantages and the drawbacks of AI.

The main weakness is that the paragraph describes AI in general instead of arguing for correct use of AI in college. It never says what correct use looks like or why it matters for students. The first two sentences cover workers and business professionals, which moves away from the college setting. The reasoning also stops at “understanding is crucial” without saying what a student should do with that understanding.

Critique
The paragraph makes observations but not a claim. A stronger version would take a position, such as correct use means using AI to support your own learning while staying honest about it. It also does not mention academic integrity, citing or disclosing AI use, or checking AI output for errors, all of which are central to using AI responsibly in a university setting.

Alternative perspective
Another way to frame the topic is to focus on the student’s role instead of AI’s capabilities. Using AI correctly in college can be seen as using it as a tutor or brainstorming partner, not as a substitute for your own thinking. Under that view, the test of correct use is whether the student still understands, can explain, and can stand behind the work they submit, and whether they are transparent about where AI helped. This keeps the paragraph focused on responsibility and the honor code, and it makes the feature you mention, tailoring responses to your needs, a benefit instead of just a description.

Incorrect AI Usage

Ghostwriting Assignments
Having AI write an entire essay or homework assignment and turning it in as your own work.

Cheating on Exams
Using AI tools during a quiz or exam when the instructor has not allowed outside help.

Fake Sources
Copying citations from AI without checking them, even though AI can invent sources that do not exist.

I think AI is a helpful tool, but using it to do your work for you is dishonest. When students let AI think for them, they miss the chance to actually learn the material. Turning in AI work as your own breaks the Aggie Honor Code and can lead to serious consequences. AI should support your learning, not replace it.
