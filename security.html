Malware and Cyber Threats

The WannaCry Ransomware Attack

On May 12, 2017, a ransomware worm called WannaCry began spreading across the world, locking files and demanding Bitcoin payments to unlock them. It infected more than 200,000 computers in over 150 countries and disrupted organizations such as hospitals in the United Kingdom, where some patient care had to be delayed. The attack spread by exploiting a flaw in older, unpatched versions of Windows. WannaCry is significant in cybersecurity history because it showed how quickly a single piece of malware can cause global damage and why keeping software updated is so important.
