This website is a class website about Management Information Systems (MIS), using AI, and the ethics that come with it. MIS is how businesses combine the use of technology, data, and people to properly run things and make decisions within the company. To learn more about MIS, you can visit the sites for Gartner, MIT Technology Review, or CIO. The Home page by Dair introduces the site and MIS, shows an image to help you understand MIS, and includes a table of different MIS topics. Lastly, Jessica has both taken the initiative as group leader and created the About Us page, where you can learn more about our team.

Welcome to our class website, which explores Management Information Systems (MIS), artificial intelligence, and the ethical questions that come with using it. MIS is the way businesses bring together technology, data, and people to run their operations smoothly and make informed decisions. If you would like to learn more about MIS, Gartner, MIT Technology Review, and CIO are excellent places to start. Dair's Home page introduces the site and the basics of MIS, featuring a helpful image and a table of key MIS topics. On the AI Usage page, Sophia and Clara explain how students should and shouldn't use artificial intelligence in college, while Luke's Security page explores hackers and cyber threats and offers tips for staying safe online. Finally, Jessica, who took the initiative as our group leader, created the About Us page, where you can get to know our team.

MIS Topic Name Description / Purpose
Cybersecurity Protects an organization's systems and data from attacks. Learn more from the NIST Cybersecurity Framework.
Database Management Organizes and stores business information like customer records, sales, and inventory so employees can quickly find and use it to make decisions.
Cloud Computing Lets businesses store data and run software over the internet instead of on their own computers, which saves money and allows employees to work from anywhere.
