Sophia Dailey

Through this assignment, I learned that Artificial Intelligence (AI) can be a very helpful tool when used responsibly and correctly. AI can be used to help students understand difficult topics, develop ideas without replacing their own thinking, and organize information. I also learned during this project that AI can help Management Information Systems (MIS) so that when businesses organize, collect, and use information they can make better decisions regarding the company. It is important when using AI that all information is double checked, since mistakes can occur. Another important takeaway is that caution should be taken when working with AI and MIS so that a businesses assets and private information is protected. Overall, I learned how AI can improve productivity and learning when it is being used in the correct and ethical way, and as a tool to improve decision-making rather than replacing it.
